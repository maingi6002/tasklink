About TaskLink

Our mission is to make remote work accessible, safe, and rewarding for everyone.

Our Story

We Believe Remote Work Should Be Stress-Free

TaskLink was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: eliminate the noise, scams, and frustration from the remote job search. We saw too many talented professionals wasting time sifting through dubious listings on traditional job boards, so we built a better way.

Our team manually researches and verifies every single job listing before it goes live. We check company legitimacy, review job descriptions for accuracy, and remove listings that don't meet our quality standards. The result is a curated marketplace where job seekers can apply with confidence.

🚀

Our Impact by the Numbers

Since our founding, we've connected over 5 million job seekers with legitimate remote opportunities at 50,000+ companies worldwide. Our platform hosts 200+ career categories, from software engineering to customer service, marketing, and beyond.

We're proud that 94% of our members say they would recommend TaskLink to a friend or colleague. Our rigorous verification process means our members spend less time worrying and more time pursuing opportunities they love.
