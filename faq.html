Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TaskLink.

What is TaskLink? ▼
TaskLink is a premium remote job platform connecting job seekers with verified remote, freelance, and flexible work opportunities. Every listing is manually reviewed to ensure it is legitimate and scam-free.
How is TaskLink different from other job boards? ▼
Unlike Indeed or LinkedIn, every job on TaskLink is manually vetted by our research team. We filter out scams, MLMs, and low-quality listings. Our platform focuses exclusively on remote and flexible work, with no ads or irrelevant postings.
How do I get started? ▼
Create a free account, complete a short skills survey, choose a plan that fits your needs, make payment, and verify your OTP. Once your plan is active, you can access tasks and apply for jobs immediately.
What plans do you offer? ▼
We offer four tiers: Free ($0) — browse jobs only, Beginner ($1) — access micro-tasks, Premium ($2) — full access to jobs under $10/hr, and Pro ($5 per job) — access to all jobs including high-paying ones paying $10+/hr.
How do payments work? ▼
We support M-Pesa (Paybill 400200, Account 1158263) and PayPal (maingi6002@gmail.com). After payment, our admin team confirms and generates a one-time OTP sent to your email. Enter the OTP on our verification page to activate your plan.
How do I earn money? ▼
Complete beginner tasks (earn $0.50-$2.00 per task) or apply for complex/pro jobs (earn $10-$5000). Our admin team reviews your submissions. Once approved, earnings are added to your wallet balance.
How do I withdraw my earnings? ▼
Minimum withdrawal is $10. We support M-Pesa, PayPal, Airtel Money, and Bank Transfer. Submit a withdrawal request from your dashboard. Admin reviews and processes payouts.
How do employers post jobs? ▼
Employers register at our Employer Portal, fill out a structured job form with full details, and submit for admin review. Once approved, the job appears on our public job board for qualified applicants.
Is my data safe? ▼
We take privacy seriously. Your personal information is stored securely and never shared with third parties without your consent. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I contact support? ▼
Email us at tasklink69@gmail.com. Our team typically responds within 24 hours.
