Career Advice & Resources

Tips, guides, and strategies for your remote job search.

Job Search

How to Find a Legitimate Remote Job

Learn how to identify legitimate remote job opportunities and avoid common scams. We share our proven framework for vetting employers and listings.

  • Research the company before applying
  • Never pay for a job guarantee
  • Look for verified listings on trusted platforms
  • Trust your instincts — if it sounds too good, it probably is
Resume Tips

Remote Resume Writing Guide

Your resume is your first impression. Learn how to tailor it for remote positions and highlight the skills employers want.

  • Highlight remote-specific skills like self-motivation
  • Showcase your home office setup and tech proficiency
  • Include measurable achievements, not just duties
  • Keep it concise — one page is ideal
Interview Prep

Ace Your Remote Job Interview

Remote interviews are different. Prepare for video calls, async assessments, and virtual culture fit evaluations.

  • Test your tech setup before the interview
  • Prepare a quiet, professional background
  • Practice answers about remote collaboration
  • Have questions ready about company culture
Freelancing

Getting Started with Freelance Work

Tips for beginners entering the freelance marketplace. Build your reputation and land your first clients.

  • Start with smaller tasks to build your profile
  • Set competitive but fair rates
  • Communicate clearly and meet deadlines
  • Request testimonials from satisfied clients
Work From Home

Setting Up a Productive Home Office

Create a workspace that maximizes focus and productivity. Simple changes make a big difference.

  • Dedicate a specific area for work
  • Invest in a good chair and lighting
  • Minimize distractions with noise-canceling headphones
  • Establish a daily routine and stick to it
Salary Tips

Negotiating Your Remote Salary

Don't leave money on the table. Learn how to research and negotiate fair compensation for remote roles.

  • Research market rates for your role and location
  • Factor in cost savings from remote work
  • Practice your negotiation conversation
  • Consider total compensation, not just salary
